# 💫 About Me:
I’m Mohammad Adnaan Gouri, a Computer Science and Engineering student specialising in Artificial Intelligence at Anand International College of Engineering, Jaipur. I’m currently in my fifth semester and expect to graduate in 2028. My main interests are backend development, AI applications and full-stack engineering. I enjoy taking an idea beyond the model or prototype and building the APIs, database and interface that make it usable.<br><br>Most of my experience comes from building projects and participating in hackathons. I work with Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL and React, and I also build Android applications using Kotlin and Jetpack Compose. Working across these areas has helped me understand how different parts of an application depend on each other, from handling data in the backend to presenting useful results to a user.<br><br>One project I’m particularly proud of is **Presence.AI**, a face recognition attendance system. My contribution covered the system architecture, data ingestion, FastAPI REST APIs, PostgreSQL schema and React dashboard. Using InsightFace and FAISS, the system reduced check-in time for batches of more than 100 students from about ten minutes to under thirty seconds. It gave me experience connecting computer vision with a complete application.<br><br>Another major project is **P2P Intelligence**, a property risk and viability platform. I worked on a Kotlin and Jetpack Compose client with a Python backend for identifying risks in property documents and assessing spatial viability using satellite data. My work included document ingestion, retrieval with pgvector and spatial analysis using YOLOv8n. The project also led to a provisional patent application submitted to the Indian Patent Office.<br><br>I’ve explored mobile development through **KavachMesh** and **Nyaya AI**. KavachMesh is an offline communication application that I built and demonstrated during HackJKLU v5.0. It uses BLE mesh networking and Noise Protocol XX encryption, with geographic restrictions and zero-trace storage. Nyaya AI is another Kotlin-based AI application that I’ve been building and iterating on.<br><br>On the web side, I built **DESH.E**, a marketplace experience for local artists and products using HTML, CSS and JavaScript. I also developed a **Voice Agent Assistant**, connecting a FastAPI backend and JavaScript interface to Murf AI’s text-to-speech API, including request handling and browser audio playback.<br><br>Hackathons have been a significant part of my learning. My achievements include a national win at **YUVA Future 6.0** with the DAWN ENGINEER team for our road-safety project, Samaritan Shield; **fourth place at The Unstoppable Hackathon at LNMIIT Jaipur** from more than 300 shortlisted teams; **fifth place at the AESTR Alpha AI Summit 2026** among more than 160 teams; and **sixth position at the JEC Kukas Hackathon**. These experiences have given me opportunities to work with teammates, explain technical choices and make decisions within a limited timeframe.<br><br>I’m looking for an engineering opportunity where I can contribute to a real product and learn from people with experience building and maintaining it. I’m particularly interested in Python backends, AI features, document-processing systems and full-stack applications. I want to become better at testing, evaluating model outputs, deployment and maintaining software after its first release.<br><br>I’m willing to make meaningful time for the right opportunity and plan my college commitments around the responsibilities we agree on. I value direct feedback and clear expectations, and I’m happy to discuss my projects or demonstrate my skills through a practical task.<br><br>Portfolio: https://adnaan-portfolio.online  <br>GitHub: https://github.com/Adnaan-786  <br>LinkedIn: https://www.linkedin.com/in/mohammad-adnaan-gouri-77389b30b
